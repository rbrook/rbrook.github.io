Privacy Policy

Coldstore · last updated 10 September 2026

Scope

Coldstore is operated by a single individual, on that individual's own hardware, against that individual's own storage account. It is not offered to other users and no other account is able to authorise it.

Data accessed

The agent uses the Google Drive API solely to write and read its own archive. It requests the narrowest scope sufficient for that task, and is confined to a directory it creates and owns. It does not enumerate, read or modify unrelated content in the storage account.

Data handling

Archive contents are deduplicated and encrypted on the operator's machine before transmission. The storage provider receives ciphertext. Encryption keys never leave the operator's control and are not recoverable by any third party, including the provider.

Transmission and storage

Data moves directly between the operator's machine and the operator's own storage account. There is no intermediate server, no hosted backend, and no telemetry or analytics of any kind. Nothing is transmitted to the author or to any third party.

Retention

Archives are retained under a policy the operator configures, and are deleted by the operator. No copy is held anywhere else.

Disclosure

None. No data is sold, shared, transferred or disclosed to any third party.

Revoking access

API access may be withdrawn at any time from Google Account permissions, which immediately invalidates the agent's credentials.
